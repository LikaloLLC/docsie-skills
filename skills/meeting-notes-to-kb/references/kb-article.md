# Knowledge base article format

**Title:** the question a reader would ask, for example "How do I reset a user's password?"

**Answer:** the first paragraph answers the question in one or two sentences.

**Steps** (only when there is a procedure): a numbered list, one action per step.

**Details** (optional): conditions, limits and exceptions mentioned in the meeting.

**Related** (optional): other questions from the same meeting that a reader may want next.

Keep each article under 300 words. Split longer topics into separate questions.
