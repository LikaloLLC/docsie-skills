# IT help desk Q&A notes (example)

- New starters asked how to get VPN access. Answer: request it in the IT portal under Access, VPN. Approval comes from your manager; the VPN profile appears in the company app within an hour of approval.
- Password resets: use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link expires after 15 minutes. If you are locked out after 5 failed attempts, wait 30 minutes or call the help desk.
- Laptops: replacement requests go through the IT portal, Hardware. Standard replacement time is 3 working days.
- Question about using personal phones for work email. Policy is still being decided; IT will confirm next month.
- Short discussion about the next Q&A session date.
