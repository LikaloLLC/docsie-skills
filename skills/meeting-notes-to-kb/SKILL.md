# Meeting notes to knowledge base

Turn meeting notes or a call transcript into short knowledge base articles that answer one question each.

## When to use

You are given meeting notes or a transcript, and a book to write into. Typical sources are support calls, onboarding sessions and internal Q&A meetings.

## Steps

1. Find every question the meeting answered, every decision that changes how people work, and every how-to that was explained.
2. Group points about the same topic. One topic becomes one article.
3. Write each article in the format in `references/kb-article.md`.
4. Save each article in the book you are given.
5. In your final message, list the articles you saved and any open questions the meeting did not answer.

## Rules

- Use only what the meeting said.
- Never save an article for a question the meeting didn't answer, or for something still being decided. Only list it under open questions in your final message.
- Leave out small talk, scheduling and who said what.
- Never include people's names, email addresses or phone numbers in an article.
- Don't create shelves or books, and don't publish.
