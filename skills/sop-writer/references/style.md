# House style for procedures

- Second person, imperative: "Press Start", not "The operator should press Start".
- One action per step. Split "Open the valve and check the gauge" into two steps.
- Name buttons and labels exactly as printed, in bold: **Start**, **E-STOP**.
- Units always with a space: 5 bar, 20 °C, 30 s.
- Warnings go in the Safety section, never inside a step.
- No more than 12 steps; group longer tasks into sub-procedures.
