# House style guide (example)

Replace these rules with your own.

1. Address the reader as "you". Don't write "the user" or "users".
2. Use the imperative in steps: "Select **Save**", not "You should select Save".
3. One action per numbered step.
4. Name buttons, menus and fields exactly as they appear on screen, in bold.
5. Use sentence case for headings: "Create a book", not "Create A Book".
6. Write numbers as digits with a space before units: 5 MB, 30 s.
7. Avoid "simply", "just", "easily" and "obviously".
8. Spell out an abbreviation the first time it appears, except well-known ones such as PDF or URL.
9. Keep sentences under 25 words where you can.
